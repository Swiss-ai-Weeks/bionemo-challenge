Sampling method and limitations
The sample uses the Articles subset of BindingDB. One exact measurement per distinct sequence–molecule–endpoint pair is selected, with no averaging. Censored labels are excluded. Query-only alignments, assay-construct differences, and possible overlap with model training data limit interpretation. Pooled correlation is not the same as ranking ligands within one target.
